How this calculator works

Enter the measurements requested above using the units shown. YardCompute keeps the calculation precise internally and rounds the displayed estimate for easier planning. Add reasonable waste where appropriate and verify final quantities against your material supplier's product specifications.

Assumptions and material notes

Results are planning estimates. Actual requirements can change because of site conditions, material dimensions, installation methods, cuts, waste, local practices, and manufacturer specifications. For structural work or unusually shaped areas, confirm the result with a qualified professional.

Frequently asked questions

Are these results exact?

No. They are estimates intended for planning and material takeoffs. Verify quantities before purchasing.

Why should I allow for waste?

Cuts, off-cuts, breakage, uneven ground, and installation details can increase the material needed. A common planning allowance is around 5–10%, but the appropriate amount depends on the project.

Can I use decimal measurements?

Yes. The calculator accepts decimal values such as 7.5 feet or 3.25 inches.

What units are supported?

The current inputs show the units used by each formula directly. Convert your measurements to those units before entering them.

What if my project has an irregular shape?

Break the project into simple sections, calculate each section separately, and add the results.

Should I verify the result?

Yes. Material packaging, local requirements, and installation conditions can change the final quantity.
