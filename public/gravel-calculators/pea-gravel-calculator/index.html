Frequently asked questions

Can I use my own measurements?

Yes. Replace the default values with your measured project dimensions before calculating.

Does this include waste?

Where provided, waste is included as an adjustable input or documented planning allowance.

Are the results exact purchase quantities?

No. They are planning estimates. Packaging, site conditions, and supplier practices can change the final amount.

What if my project is irregular?

Break it into simple measurable sections, calculate each section, and combine the results where appropriate.

What should I verify before ordering?

Verify measurements, product specifications, installation requirements, and supplier yield or coverage information.
