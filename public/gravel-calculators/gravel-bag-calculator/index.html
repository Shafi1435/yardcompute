How many bags of gravel do I need?

For small paths, planters, and drainage spots, bagged gravel is often easier than a bulk delivery. This calculator finds the volume of gravel for a rectangular area at your chosen depth, adds a waste allowance, and divides by the volume in one bag.

How to use the gravel bag calculator

  1. Enter the length and width of the area in feet.
  2. Enter the gravel depth in inches.
  3. Enter the bag size in cubic feet, as printed on the bag. 0.5 cu ft is a common size.
  4. Set a waste allowance and select Calculate.

Formula

Volume (cu ft) = length × width × (depth ÷ 12)
Volume with waste = volume × (1 + waste % ÷ 100)
Cubic yards = volume with waste ÷ 27
Bags = round up (volume with waste ÷ bag size)

Example

The default values describe a 20 ft × 10 ft area covered 3 in deep, using 0.5 cu ft bags. The area is 200 sq ft, and the base volume is 200 × 3/12 = 50 cu ft. With 10% extra, that is 55 cu ft, or 2.04 cubic yards, which takes 110 bags.

At 2 in deep, the same area needs 36.67 cu ft with waste, or 74 bags.

When bulk gravel makes more sense

The bag count rises fast. As a rough guide, one cubic yard is 27 cu ft, which is 54 half-cubic-foot bags. Once a project needs more than a cubic yard or two, compare the bag price with a bulk delivery from a landscape supplier, and ask about delivery fees and minimums.

Choosing a depth

  • Decorative top dressing over landscape fabric is often 2–3 in.
  • Walkways usually need a compacted base under the top layer for a firm surface.
  • Driveways need much deeper, layered gravel. Use the Driveway Gravel Calculator.

Frequently asked questions

Are gravel bags sold by volume or weight?

Both are common. If the bag lists only weight, check the packaging or the store listing for its volume, or convert using the Gravel Tonnage Calculator.

Why add waste?

Gravel settles, some gets lost into the soil, and the ground is rarely perfectly flat. A 5–10% allowance covers that.

Can I use this for pea gravel or river rock?

Yes, as long as you know the bag volume. The Pea Gravel Calculator and River Rock Calculator add weight estimates.

How do I handle a curved bed?

Break it into rectangles, or measure the longest length and average width for a rough estimate.

Can I use metric?

Yes. Switch length and width to meters and depth to centimeters. Bag size stays in cubic feet.
