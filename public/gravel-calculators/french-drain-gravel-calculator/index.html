How this calculator works

Enter measured project dimensions and planning inputs. The calculator converts them into the quantities shown in the results.

Formula

Gravel volume = trench length × trench width × trench depth, converting inch dimensions to feet. The order volume then applies the selected waste allowance.

Example

For a 40 ft trench that is 12 in wide and 18 in deep, the base trench volume is 60 cu ft before waste.

Assumptions and material notes

The calculator estimates the full rectangular trench volume as gravel. Actual designs may reserve space for pipe, fabric, fittings, or other materials.

Frequently asked questions

Does this account for the drain pipe?

No. It calculates the trench volume from the dimensions entered.

What density should I use?

Use supplier or product density when available; the default is only a planning value.

Can I change waste?

Yes. Enter a project-appropriate waste allowance.

Can I use metric units?

Use the unit choices provided by the calculator fields.

Should I verify quantity?

Yes. Confirm trench dimensions, pipe space, material type, and supplier coverage before ordering.
