How much river rock do I need?

River rock is smooth, rounded stone used in beds, dry creek beds, borders, and around downspouts. This calculator finds the volume of rock for a rectangular area at your depth, adds waste, and estimates the weight so you can compare bulk and bagged prices.

How to use the river rock calculator

  1. Enter the length and width of the area in feet.
  2. Enter the rock depth in inches. Larger stones usually need a deeper layer to cover the ground.
  3. Enter the rock density in pounds per cubic foot, or keep the default if your supplier has no figure.
  4. Set a waste allowance and select Calculate.

Formula

Area = length × width
Volume with waste (cu ft) = area × (depth ÷ 12) × (1 + waste % ÷ 100)
Cubic yards = volume ÷ 27
Tons = volume × density ÷ 2,000

Example

The default values describe a 20 ft × 10 ft area, 2 in deep, with rock at 100 lb per cu ft. The area is 200 sq ft. With 10% extra, the volume is 36.67 cu ft, or 1.36 cubic yards, weighing about 1.83 tons.

At 3 in deep, the same bed needs 55 cu ft with waste, or 2.04 cubic yards and about 2.75 tons.

Choosing a depth for river rock

A useful rule is to make the layer at least as deep as the stones are wide, and often a little deeper, so the ground does not show between rocks. Small stones may cover well at 2 in, while 2–3 in stones usually need 3–4 in. For dry creek beds with large rocks, estimate the large stones separately by count.

Tips

  • Lay landscape fabric first to slow weeds and stop rock sinking into soil.
  • Install edging so rock stays out of lawn and paths.
  • River rock is heavy. A cubic yard can weigh well over a ton, so plan how you will move it.

Frequently asked questions

Is river rock the same as pea gravel?

Both are rounded, but pea gravel is small, around pea size, while river rock is usually larger. Use the Pea Gravel Calculator for pea gravel.

How many bags of river rock is this?

Divide the volume in cubic feet by the bag volume. A 0.5 cu ft bag is a common size, so 36.67 cu ft is about 74 bags.

Why is density only an estimate?

Stone type, size, and moisture change the weight. Ask your supplier for their figure for the most accurate tonnage.

Can I use metric?

Yes. Switch dimensions to meters and centimeters and density to kg/m³.

How do I measure an irregular bed?

Break it into rectangles, or use the longest length and average width for a quick estimate.
