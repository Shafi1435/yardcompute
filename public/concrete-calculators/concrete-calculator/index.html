How the concrete calculator works

Enter the length, width, and thickness of a rectangular slab, pad, or footing. The calculator converts thickness from inches to feet and estimates volume.

Formula

Volume = length × width × thickness in feet
Order volume = volume × 1.10
Cubic yards = order volume ÷ 27

Example

A 10 ft × 10 ft slab at 4 inches thick is about 33.33 cubic feet, or about 1.36 cubic yards after the built-in 10% planning allowance.

Assumptions and material notes

The 10% allowance is a general planning estimate, not a universal waste factor. Slopes, uneven subgrade, forms, over-excavation, and supplier minimums can change the order quantity.

Frequently asked questions

Why add 10%?

It provides a simple planning allowance for minor variation and waste. Your project may need more or less.

How many cubic yards are in a cubic foot?

One cubic yard equals 27 cubic feet.

Does this calculate ready-mix truck loads?

No. Confirm truck minimums and delivery requirements with your supplier.

Can I use decimal dimensions?

Yes.

Should I verify the order quantity with my supplier?

Yes. Confirm the final quantity, minimum order, delivery requirements, and mix specifications with your concrete supplier.
