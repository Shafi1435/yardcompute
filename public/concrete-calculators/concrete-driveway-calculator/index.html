Concrete Driveway Calculator

Estimate concrete volume and cubic yards for a rectangular driveway using slab dimensions and a waste allowance.

Enter your measurements

Enter your project measurements and select Calculate.

How this calculator works

Enter measured project dimensions and planning inputs. The calculator converts them into the quantities shown in the results.

Formula

Concrete volume = length × width × slab thickness in feet. Order volume applies the selected waste allowance, then cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27.

Example

For a 40 ft × 12 ft driveway at 5 in thick, the base volume is 200 cu ft before waste.

Assumptions and material notes

This is a rectangular slab planning estimate. It does not design reinforcement, subbase, joints, slopes, or thickened edges.

Frequently asked questions

What thickness should I enter?

Enter the planned slab thickness from your project design or specifications.

Does it include waste?

Yes. The waste field is applied to the base concrete volume.

How are cubic yards calculated?

Cubic feet are divided by 27.

Does it calculate reinforcement?

No. Reinforcement and structural details are outside this volume estimate.

Should I verify the order?

Yes. Recheck measurements and confirm the final quantity with your supplier.
