Concrete calculator for post holes: quick answer

This post hole concrete calculator estimates the cylindrical volume of concrete needed for one or more holes and converts the result to an approximate bag count using the selected product yield.

For one 12-inch-diameter hole that is 24 inches deep:

π × 6² × 24 ÷ 1,728 = about 1.57 cubic feet

With the calculator's 10% planning allowance, that is about 1.73 cubic feet per hole. If your concrete product yields 0.60 cubic feet per bag, the planning estimate is about 2.88 bags, or 3 bags per hole. Always use the yield printed on your specific product when ordering.

How the post hole concrete calculator works

Enter the number of holes, hole diameter, and hole depth. The calculator estimates cylindrical concrete volume and applies a 10% planning allowance before showing the material estimate.

Formula for post-hole concrete

Hole volume = π × radius² × depth

When dimensions are entered in inches, convert the resulting cubic inches to cubic feet by dividing by 1,728.

Order volume = total hole volume × 1.10

Bags = order volume ÷ product yield per bag

Example: concrete for four post holes

Four 12-inch-diameter holes at 24 inches deep have about 6.28 cubic feet of cylindrical volume before the planning allowance. With 10%, the estimate is about 6.91 cubic feet. At a 0.60 cu ft yield per bag, that is about 11.52 bags, so 12 bags for planning.

Important assumptions

The calculator does not subtract the volume displaced by the post. That can make the estimate conservative. Actual concrete placement also depends on hole shape, excavation conditions, installation method, and the product being used.

Bag yields are not universal. Different concrete products can produce different volumes from the same bag size, so use the manufacturer's published yield instead of assuming every 80-lb bag has the same yield.

Post-hole concrete vs. fence-post concrete

Use this calculator when you are focused specifically on cylindrical post-hole volume and concrete quantity. If you are planning an entire fence and also need posts, sections, spacing, and other fence materials, use the Fence Calculator and the Fence Post Concrete Calculator together.

Frequently asked questions

How much concrete do I need for one post hole?

It depends on hole diameter and depth. A 12-inch-diameter hole 24 inches deep is about 1.57 cubic feet before the planning allowance.

How many bags of concrete do I need per post hole?

Divide the adjusted hole volume by the concrete product's published yield per bag and round up for purchasing. For example, a 12-inch × 24-inch hole with a 10% allowance and 0.60 cu ft/bag yield is about 3 bags.

Does this include a waste allowance?

Yes. The main estimate includes a 10% planning allowance.

Does it subtract the post volume?

No. It estimates the full cylindrical hole volume, which can be conservative when the post occupies part of the hole.

Can I use different hole sizes?

Calculate each hole size separately when your project has multiple hole diameters or depths.

Are concrete bag yields universal?

No. Always use the yield printed on the specific concrete product you plan to buy.

What hole size should I use?

Use the hole dimensions required by your post size, soil conditions, frost depth, structural requirements, and local installation guidance.
