How much sand do I need under pavers?

Pavers are usually set on a thin, smooth layer of bedding sand that sits on top of a compacted gravel base. This calculator estimates the volume of that bedding layer from the project area and sand depth, with a waste allowance.

How to use the paver sand calculator

  1. Enter the length and width of the paved area in feet.
  2. Enter the bedding sand depth in inches. About 1 in is a common bedding depth, but follow the paver manufacturer's instructions.
  3. Set a waste allowance and select Calculate.

Formula

Area = length × width
Sand with waste (cu ft) = area × (depth ÷ 12) × (1 + waste % ÷ 100)
Cubic yards = sand ÷ 27

Example

The default values describe a 20 ft × 12 ft patio with 1 in of bedding sand. The area is 240 sq ft, and the base volume is 240 × 1/12 = 20 cu ft. With 10% extra, plan for 22 cu ft, or about 0.81 cubic yards.

Bedding sand vs. joint sand

These are two different materials and two different jobs:

  • Bedding sand is the screeded layer under the pavers. Coarse, sharp concrete sand is commonly used. This calculator estimates it.
  • Joint sand fills the gaps between pavers after they are laid. It is often a fine or polymeric sand. Use the Polymeric Sand Calculator for that.
  • Base gravel goes under the bedding sand and is much thicker. Use the Paver Base and Bedding Sand Calculator to estimate both layers together.

Why not use more sand?

A thick sand layer can shift and settle unevenly, leaving dips in the finished surface. The strength of a paver surface comes from the compacted base; the sand is there to give a smooth, even bed. Keep bedding sand to the depth your paver manufacturer recommends.

Frequently asked questions

How many bags of sand is 22 cu ft?

Divide by the bag volume. Sand bags are often sold by weight, so check the bag or store listing for its volume. For larger jobs, bulk sand is usually cheaper.

Can I use play sand?

Play sand is usually too fine and rounded for bedding. Coarse, angular sand holds its shape better. Check what your paver manufacturer recommends.

Do I need to compact the sand?

Bedding sand is screeded level, not compacted first. It is compacted when the pavers are laid and the surface is run over with a plate compactor.

Can I use metric?

Yes. Switch length and width to meters and depth to centimeters.
