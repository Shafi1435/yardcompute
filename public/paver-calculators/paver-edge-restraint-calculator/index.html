How much paver edge restraint do I need?

Edge restraint is the plastic, aluminum, steel, or concrete edging that holds pavers in place so they do not spread apart over time. It runs along the open edges of a paved area. This calculator finds the perimeter of a rectangular patio or walkway, adds a waste allowance, and rounds up to whole linear feet.

How to use the paver edge restraint calculator

  1. Enter the length and width of the paved area in feet.
  2. Set a waste allowance for cuts, overlaps, and corners.
  3. Select Calculate.

Formula

Perimeter = 2 × (length + width)
Edge restraint with waste = perimeter × (1 + waste % ÷ 100)
Order quantity = round up (edge restraint with waste)

Example

The default values describe a 20 ft × 12 ft patio. The perimeter is 2 × (20 + 12) = 64 ft. With 10% extra, that is 70.4 ft, so the calculator suggests 71 linear feet.

Adjust for edges that are already held

The calculator assumes all four sides need edging. Sides that butt against a house foundation, a concrete slab, a wall, or steps are already restrained. Subtract those lengths. For example, if one 20 ft side of the patio runs along the house, the open edges total 44 ft, or 48.4 ft with 10% extra.

Converting to pieces and spikes

Edging is sold in set lengths, often several feet per piece. Divide the result by the piece length and round up. Most edging is held down with spikes; the spacing depends on the product, so check the manufacturer's instructions for how many to buy.

Curves

Flexible edging can follow curves. For a curved edge, measure along the curve with a flexible tape or a rope, then use that length instead of the rectangle perimeter.

Frequently asked questions

Do I really need edge restraint?

For most sand-set paver patios and walkways, yes. Without it, the edge pavers can creep outward and the joints open up.

Which type should I use?

Plastic and metal edging are common for residential patios. Heavier-duty or concrete edges may suit driveways. Follow the paver manufacturer's recommendation.

Is edge restraint installed before or after the pavers?

Practices vary. It is often installed after the pavers are laid and the edges cut. Follow the edging product's instructions.

Can I use metric?

Yes. Switch length and width to meters; the result shows linear feet.
