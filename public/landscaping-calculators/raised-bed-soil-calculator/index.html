How much soil do I need for a raised bed?

A raised bed is a box, so the soil it needs is length × width × depth. This calculator works that out for one or more beds of the same size, adds a waste allowance for settling, and converts the total to cubic yards for bulk orders.

How to use the raised bed soil calculator

  1. Enter the inside length and width of the bed in feet.
  2. Enter the soil depth in inches. Measure to the top of the soil, not the top of the bed walls.
  3. Enter the number of beds with the same size.
  4. Set a waste allowance and select Calculate.

Formula

Soil volume (cu ft) = length × width × (depth ÷ 12) × beds
Volume with allowance = soil volume × (1 + waste % ÷ 100)
Cubic yards = volume ÷ 27

Example

The default values describe one 8 ft × 4 ft bed filled 12 in deep. That is 32 cu ft of soil, or 35.2 cu ft with a 10% allowance, which is about 1.30 cubic yards.

Three of the same beds need 105.6 cu ft with the allowance, or about 3.91 cubic yards.

Bags or bulk?

Bagged soil is often sold in 1, 1.5, or 2 cu ft bags. One 8 × 4 ft bed at 12 in deep needs 35.2 cu ft, which is about 18 bags of 2 cu ft. For several beds, bulk soil delivered by the yard is usually cheaper per cubic foot. Ask your supplier about delivery minimums.

Planning tips

  • New soil settles, especially in deep beds. The allowance helps, and many gardeners top up each season.
  • Deep beds can be partly filled with coarse organic material at the bottom, which reduces the soil needed. Subtract that depth if you plan to do it.
  • Many gardeners use a mix of topsoil and compost rather than one material. Use the total volume and split it by your mix ratio.

Frequently asked questions

How deep should a raised bed be?

It depends on what you grow. Many vegetables do well in 8–12 in of soil, while deep-rooted crops benefit from more. Check guidance for your plants.

Can I calculate round or L-shaped beds?

For a round bed, use π × radius² × depth. For other shapes, split them into rectangles and add the volumes.

Should I measure inside or outside the frame?

Inside. The frame boards take up space that will not hold soil.

Can I use metric?

Yes. Switch length and width to meters and depth to centimeters.
