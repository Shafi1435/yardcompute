Grass Seed Calculator

Estimate grass seed quantity from lawn area and the product seeding rate.

How much grass seed do I need?

Enter your lawn length and width, then use the seeding rate printed on your grass seed product. The calculator estimates the base seed amount and adds your selected waste allowance.

Formula

Lawn area = length × width
Base seed = lawn area × seed rate ÷ 1,000
Seed to order = base seed × (1 + waste ÷ 100)

Example

For a 40 ft × 20 ft lawn, the area is 800 sq ft. At a product rate of 5 lb per 1,000 sq ft, the base requirement is 4 lb. With a 10% allowance, the planning quantity is 4.4 lb.

Use the seed product rate

Seeding rates vary by grass type, product, and whether you are establishing a new lawn or overseeding. Use the rate on the product label rather than a generic rate.

Assumptions and material notes

Results are planning estimates. Exclude driveways, patios, planting beds, and other areas that will not be seeded. For irregular lawns, divide the area into simple shapes and combine the measurements.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my own measurements?

Yes. Replace the default values with your measured project dimensions before calculating.

Does this include waste?

If the calculator includes a waste allowance, you can adjust it to match your project and product.

Are the results exact purchase quantities?

No. They are planning estimates. Packaging, coverage, cuts, and site conditions can change the final amount.

What if my project is irregular?

Break the project into simple measurable sections, calculate each section, and combine the results where appropriate.

What should I verify before ordering?

Verify measurements, product specifications, coverage or yield information, installation requirements, and any local requirements.
