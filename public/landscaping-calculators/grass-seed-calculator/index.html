How much grass seed do I need?

Enter your lawn length and width, then use the seeding rate printed on your grass seed product. The calculator estimates the base seed amount and adds your selected waste allowance.

How to use the grass seed calculator

  1. Measure the lawn area you actually plan to seed.
  2. Exclude patios, driveways, planting beds, buildings, and other non-lawn areas.
  3. Enter the product's recommended seeding rate.
  4. Choose a reasonable allowance for your project, enter the bag size you plan to buy, and review the pounds and approximate bags to order.
  5. For irregular lawns, divide the space into simple rectangles and combine the areas.

Formula

Lawn area = length × width
Base seed = lawn area × seed rate ÷ 1,000
Seed to order = base seed × (1 + waste ÷ 100)
Bags to order = ceiling(seed to order ÷ bag size)

Example

For a 40 ft × 20 ft lawn, the area is 800 sq ft. At a product rate of 5 lb per 1,000 sq ft, the base requirement is 4 lb. With a 10% allowance, the planning quantity is 4.4 lb. If the product is sold in 20-lb bags, that is approximately 1 bag.

Use the seed product rate

Seeding rates vary by grass type, product, seed blend, and whether you are establishing a new lawn or overseeding. Use the rate on the product label whenever possible instead of relying on a generic rate.

New lawn vs. overseeding

New lawn establishment and overseeding can use different recommended rates. The correct quantity depends on the seed product and the job. If the label gives separate rates, enter the rate that matches your intended use.

Common planning mistakes

  • Calculating the whole property instead of the actual seedable lawn area.
  • Using a generic seed rate instead of the product label.
  • Forgetting that irregular areas need to be divided into measurable sections.
  • Ordering by bag size without checking the actual pounds per bag.
  • Assuming the calculated quantity guarantees coverage when site preparation or application conditions differ.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my own measurements?

Yes. Replace the default values with your measured project dimensions before calculating.

Does this include waste?

Yes. The calculator applies the percentage you enter to the base seed requirement.

What seed rate should I use?

Use the recommended rate printed on your seed product. Rates vary by grass type, product, and whether you are planting a new lawn or overseeding.

What if my lawn is irregular?

Break it into simple rectangles or other measurable sections, calculate each area, and add the areas together before applying the seed rate.

Should I use a different rate for overseeding?

Often the product label provides a separate overseeding rate. Follow the rate for the specific use case.

Are the results exact purchase quantities?

No. They are planning estimates. Packaging, product coverage, application conditions, and site preparation can change the final amount.

How does the bag estimate work?

Enter the actual bag size you plan to buy. The calculator rounds the required seed up to a whole number of bags, so you can compare the estimated pounds with the package size.

Can the calculator use metric measurements?

The length and width fields support metric units. The calculator converts them to the internal square-foot basis used with the seed-rate input.

What should I verify before ordering?

Verify lawn area, seed product, recommended rate, bag size, application instructions, and any local growing recommendations.
