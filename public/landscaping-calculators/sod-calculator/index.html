How much sod do I need?

Sod is sold by area, usually by the square foot, piece, or pallet. This calculator finds the area of a rectangular lawn, adds an allowance for cutting around edges and curves, and rounds up to a whole square foot so you can compare supplier quotes.

How to use the sod calculator

  1. Measure the lawn length and width in feet.
  2. Set a waste allowance for edges and cuts.
  3. Select Calculate, then convert the area to pieces or pallets using your supplier's figures.

Formula

Lawn area = length × width
Sod area with waste = lawn area × (1 + waste % ÷ 100)
Sod to order = round up (sod area with waste)

Example

The default values describe a 40 ft × 20 ft lawn. The area is 800 sq ft, and with 10% extra the calculator suggests 880 sq ft of sod.

Converting to pieces and pallets

Piece and pallet sizes vary by farm and grass type, so always ask your supplier. As an example, if each piece covers 10 sq ft, 880 sq ft is 88 pieces. If a pallet covers 450 sq ft, 880 sq ft is just under two pallets, so you would likely order two.

Choosing a waste allowance

  • Simple rectangles: about 5% is often enough.
  • Curved beds, trees, paths, and many edges: 10–15% is safer.
  • Sod must be laid soon after delivery, so running short can be a problem. A little extra is usually worth it.

Prepare before the sod arrives

Sod is perishable. Have the soil graded, loosened, and ready before delivery, and plan to lay it the same day if possible. Many lawns benefit from a layer of topsoil first; use the Topsoil Calculator to estimate it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure an irregular lawn?

Split it into rectangles and other simple shapes, calculate each one, and add the areas. A slightly generous estimate is safer than a short one.

Is sod better than seed?

Sod gives an instant lawn but costs more. Seed is cheaper but takes longer to establish. For seed, use the Grass Seed Calculator.

Should I include areas under trees?

Only if grass will grow there. Deep shade is often better suited to shade-tolerant grass or ground cover.

Can I use metric?

Yes. Switch length and width to meters; results show square feet.

Does this include delivery or soil prep?

No. It only estimates sod area.
