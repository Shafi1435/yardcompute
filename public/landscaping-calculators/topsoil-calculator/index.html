How much topsoil do I need?

Topsoil is usually sold by the cubic yard in bulk or by the cubic foot in bags. This calculator finds the volume for a rectangular area at your depth, adds an allowance for settling, and gives the result in both cubic feet and cubic yards.

How to use the topsoil calculator

  1. Enter the length and width of the area in feet.
  2. Enter the topsoil depth in inches.
  3. Set a waste allowance and select Calculate.

Formula

Volume (cu ft) = length × width × (depth ÷ 12)
Topsoil with waste = volume × (1 + waste % ÷ 100)
Cubic yards = topsoil ÷ 27

Example

The default values describe a 20 ft × 10 ft area covered 4 in deep. The base volume is 200 × 4/12 = 66.67 cu ft. With 10% extra, that is 73.33 cu ft, or about 2.72 cubic yards.

At 6 in deep, the same area needs 110 cu ft with the allowance, or about 4.07 cubic yards.

Common topsoil depths

  • Top dressing a lawn: a very thin layer, often well under an inch, spread over existing grass.
  • Before seed or sod: a few inches of good soil helps new grass root.
  • Filling low spots or new beds: deeper layers, depending on the grade you need.
  • These are general ranges. Your soil, plants, and local conditions should guide the final depth.

Settling and ordering

Loose topsoil settles after it is spread, watered, and walked on, so the finished layer ends up thinner than the loose depth. The allowance helps. Bulk suppliers usually sell in whole or half cubic yards, so round up to the supplier's increment.

Frequently asked questions

How many bags of topsoil is a cubic yard?

A cubic yard is 27 cu ft. With 1 cu ft bags, that is 27 bags; with 0.75 cu ft bags, it is 36.

How much does a cubic yard weigh?

It varies a lot with moisture and soil type, but a cubic yard of topsoil commonly weighs around a ton or more. Check vehicle payload limits before hauling it yourself.

Should I use topsoil or garden soil?

Topsoil is a general-purpose fill. Beds for plants often do better with a mix that includes compost. Ask your supplier what they offer.

Can I use metric?

Yes. Switch length and width to meters and depth to centimeters.
