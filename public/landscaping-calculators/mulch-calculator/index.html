How the mulch calculator works

Enter the garden bed length, width, and mulch depth. The calculator estimates cubic feet, adds 10% for planning, and converts the result to cubic yards.

Formula

Volume = length × width × depth in feet
Order volume = volume × 1.10
Cubic yards = order volume ÷ 27

Example

A 20 ft × 8 ft bed at 3 inches deep is about 40 cubic feet before the allowance, or about 1.63 cubic yards with the 10% planning allowance.

Assumptions and material notes

Mulch settles over time, and actual coverage depends on material, moisture, and application method. Bagged mulch should be checked against the manufacturer's stated coverage.

How to use the mulch calculator

  1. Measure the length and width of the bed in feet.
  2. Enter the mulch depth in inches. 2–3 in is a common range for many garden beds.
  3. Select Calculate to see the volume and cubic yards with a 10% allowance.

More examples

The default 20 ft × 8 ft bed at 3 in deep needs 40 cu ft, or 44 cu ft with the allowance. At 2 in deep, the same bed needs about 29.33 cu ft with the allowance, or about 1.09 cubic yards. A 4 ft × 50 ft border at 3 in needs about 55 cu ft, or 2.04 cubic yards.

Choosing a depth

  • A thin layer may not block weeds well; a very thick layer can hold too much moisture against plants.
  • Keep mulch pulled back from tree trunks and plant stems.
  • When refreshing existing mulch, you may only need to top up an inch or so. Measure what is already there before ordering.

Bags or bulk?

Bagged mulch is commonly 2 or 3 cu ft per bag. 44 cu ft is 22 bags of 2 cu ft or 15 bags of 3 cu ft. For larger jobs, bulk mulch by the cubic yard is often cheaper. The Mulch Bag Calculator converts volume to bags for you.

Frequently asked questions

Why add 10%?

It is a general planning allowance for settling and small measurement variation.

How many cubic feet are in a cubic yard?

One cubic yard equals 27 cubic feet.

Does this calculate bags?

No. Use the cubic-foot or cubic-yard result with the coverage printed on your mulch bag.

Can I use decimal depths?

Yes.

How should I handle irregular beds?

Break an irregular bed into simple measurable sections, calculate each section, and add the volumes together.

Can I enter metric sizes?

Yes. Switch length and width to meters and depth to centimeters.
