Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Overview

YardCompute respects your privacy. This policy explains the information that may be processed when you visit yardcompute.com and use our calculators.

Calculator inputs

The calculators are designed to perform calculations in your browser. We do not need your project measurements to provide the basic calculator results, and we do not intentionally collect those measurements as personal information.

Server and security information

Our hosting and security providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, timestamps, and security-related request data as part of delivering and protecting the website.

Analytics and cookies

If analytics or other measurement tools are enabled on the website in the future, this policy will be updated to describe the specific provider, information collected, purpose, and available choices. YardCompute does not currently promise that every browser-based technology or third-party service will be cookie-free.

Third-party services

Links to third-party websites may be provided for useful resources. Those websites have their own privacy practices and policies. Review their policies before providing them with personal information.

Children

YardCompute is a general-audience website and is not directed specifically to children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website, analytics, advertising, or other services change. The date above indicates the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method made available on the YardCompute website.
