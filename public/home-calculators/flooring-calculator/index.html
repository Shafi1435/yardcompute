How much flooring do I need?

Flooring such as laminate, vinyl plank, and engineered wood is sold in boxes, and each box covers a set number of square feet. This calculator finds the floor area of a rectangular room, adds a waste allowance for cuts, and divides by the coverage of one box.

How to use the flooring calculator

  1. Measure the room length and width in feet at the widest points.
  2. Enter the coverage per box in square feet, from the box label or product page.
  3. Set a waste allowance and select Calculate.

Formula

Floor area = length × width
Area to order = floor area × (1 + waste % ÷ 100)
Boxes = round up (area to order ÷ coverage per box)

Example

The default values describe a 16 ft × 12 ft room and flooring that covers 20 sq ft per box. The floor area is 192 sq ft. With 10% extra, that is 211.2 sq ft, and 211.2 ÷ 20 = 10.56, so you need 11 boxes.

Choosing a waste allowance

  • Straight plank or tile layouts in a simple room: about 5–10% is a common planning range.
  • Diagonal or herringbone layouts: usually more, because almost every piece at the edge is cut.
  • Many closets, doorways, or angled walls: add a little more.
  • Many installers keep a spare box for future repairs, since the same color batch can be hard to find later.

Measuring tips

For an L-shaped room, split it into two rectangles and add the areas. Include closets that will get the same floor. Do not subtract the area under cabinets or appliances unless you are sure the flooring will not go there; floating floors usually stop at cabinets, but glued floors sometimes run under them.

Frequently asked questions

Does this work for carpet?

Carpet is sold in rolls of a fixed width, so the layout and seams change the amount needed. Use the area as a starting point and ask the carpet supplier for a roll layout.

Should I include underlayment?

Underlayment is separate. It is usually sold by area, so you can use the area to order from this calculator.

Why round up to a whole box?

Most stores sell flooring by the full box, so even a small shortfall means buying another box.

Can I use metric?

Yes. Switch length and width to meters. Coverage stays in square feet, as printed on most US boxes.

How do I calculate tile instead?

Use the Tile Calculator, which works from tile size.
