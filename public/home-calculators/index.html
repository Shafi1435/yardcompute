Which home calculator do I need?

  • Painting a room: start with the Paint Calculator for wall gallons after doors, windows, and coats.
  • New walls or a remodel: the Wall Framing Calculator estimates studs and plates, then the Drywall Calculator turns the wall area into sheets.
  • New floors: use the Flooring Calculator for plank or box-sold flooring, or the Tile Calculator when you know the tile size.
  • Steps and stairs: the Stair Stringer Calculator works out riser count, riser height, run, and stringer length.

Measuring tips for indoor projects

Measure each wall or floor at its longest points and round up to the nearest inch. Split L-shaped rooms into rectangles and add the results. Write down door and window sizes as you go, because the paint and drywall calculators subtract them. For stairs, always measure total rise from finished floor to finished floor.
