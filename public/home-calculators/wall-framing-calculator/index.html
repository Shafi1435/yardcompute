Wall Framing Calculator

Estimate wall studs, plates, and framing linear footage from wall length, height, and stud spacing.

How this calculator works

Enter measured project dimensions and planning inputs. The calculator converts them into the quantities shown in the results.

Formula

Stud count = ceiling(wall length ÷ stud spacing) + 1. The calculator estimates three plate runs and applies the waste allowance to studs and plate linear footage.

Example

For a 20 ft wall at 16 in stud spacing, the calculator estimates the basic stud count and three plate runs before waste.

Assumptions and material notes

This is a basic straight-wall material estimate. It does not account for doors, windows, headers, corners, intersections, cripple studs, blocking, or structural requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Does this design doors and windows?

No. Doors and windows require separate header, king-stud, jack-stud, and opening details.

How many plates are included?

The calculator estimates three plate runs for a basic wall.

Can I change stud spacing?

Yes. Enter the target spacing for your project.

Does this design a structural wall?

No. It is a material-planning calculator, not structural engineering or code compliance.

What should I verify?

Verify framing dimensions, loads, openings, lumber sizes, local code, and construction details before building.
