About YardCompute

Free calculators for planning common home and yard projects.

What YardCompute does

YardCompute provides practical calculators for homeowners, DIYers, contractors, and property owners who need quick planning estimates for projects such as fences, decks, concrete, gravel, and landscaping.

Our approach

Each calculator is designed around an explicit calculation method, practical inputs, clear assumptions, and worked examples. Results are presented as planning estimates rather than purchase orders or engineering specifications.

How our content is created

YardCompute may use software and AI-assisted tools during drafting and editing, but the site is built around useful calculator functionality, explicit formulas, practical examples, and editorial checking of published material. We avoid publishing large numbers of near-identical pages simply to target search queries.

The goal is to help a visitor complete a real project-planning task, not to create content solely for search traffic.

Important limitation

Project conditions vary. Material dimensions, waste, installation methods, site conditions, manufacturer specifications, and local requirements can change the final quantity needed. Always verify important measurements and material quantities before purchasing or building.
