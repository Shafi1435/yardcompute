How many deck joists do I need?

Deck joists are the framing members that run under the decking and carry it to the beams and ledger. This calculator counts the joists needed across the deck width at your chosen on-center spacing, then multiplies by the joist length to give the linear footage you would order, with and without a waste allowance.

How to use the deck joist calculator

  1. Enter the deck length as the direction the joists run, usually from the house ledger out to the beam.
  2. Enter the deck width as the distance the joists are spread across, usually along the house wall.
  3. Enter the joist spacing on center. 16 in is common; 12 in and 24 in are also used depending on decking and design.
  4. Set a waste allowance and select Calculate.

Formula

Joists = round up ((deck width × 12) ÷ joist spacing) + 1
Base linear feet = joists × deck length
Linear feet with waste = base linear feet × (1 + waste % ÷ 100)

The extra joist (+1) is there because a run of evenly spaced joists always needs one more joist than the number of spaces between them. It covers the closing joist at the far end.

Example

The default values describe a 16 ft long × 12 ft wide deck with joists at 16 in on center. 12 ft is 144 in, and 144 ÷ 16 = 9 spaces, so the deck needs 9 + 1 = 10 joists. Each joist is 16 ft long, so the base requirement is 160 linear feet. With 10% extra, plan for about 176 linear feet. The deck area is 192 sq ft.

What is not included

  • Rim joists and band boards around the outside of the frame.
  • Doubled joists under stairs, railing posts, or butt joints in the decking.
  • Blocking between joists, beams, ledger boards, posts, and joist hangers.
  • Joist size. This calculator counts joists; it does not tell you whether a 2×8, 2×10, or 2×12 can span the distance. Check a span table, your local building department, or a qualified professional for that.

Choosing a joist spacing

Spacing is set by the decking you choose and by local code. Many composite and PVC decking products specify a maximum joist spacing, and diagonal decking often needs closer spacing than straight decking. Always check the decking manufacturer's installation instructions before you settle on a spacing.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between this and the Deck Joist Spacing Calculator?

This calculator focuses on how much joist lumber to buy. The Deck Joist Spacing Calculator focuses on layout: it shows the actual even spacing you get once the joist count is rounded up.

Should I buy joists longer than the deck length?

Often, yes. Lumber comes in standard lengths, so round each joist up to the next available length. Use the deck length as a minimum and account for overhangs or cantilevers in your plan.

Does 16 in on center mean 16 in between boards?

No. On-center spacing is measured from the center of one joist to the center of the next, not the clear gap between them.

Can I use this for a ground-level deck?

Yes. The joist count works the same way, but low decks may have different framing, ventilation, and lumber-rating needs. Check the lumber rating for ground contact where required.

Is this result approved for a permit?

No. It is a material planning estimate. Permit drawings need joist size, span, beam, and footing details that follow your local building code.
