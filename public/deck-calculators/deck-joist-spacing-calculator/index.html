Frequently asked questions

Can I use my own measurements?

Yes. Replace the default values with your measured project dimensions before calculating.

Does this include waste?

No separate waste allowance is included on this calculator.

Are the results exact purchase quantities?

No. They are planning estimates. Product packaging and site conditions can change the final amount.

What if my project is irregular?

Break the project into simple measurable sections, calculate each section, and combine the results where appropriate.

What should I verify before ordering?

Verify dimensions, product specifications, installation requirements, and supplier yield or coverage information.
